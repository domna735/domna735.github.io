UGOT Robotics Competition Training (7-lesson series)

A complete curriculum covering engineering fundamentals, manipulation, throwing mechanics, and match strategy.

Lesson 1 — Engineering robot familiarisation (free exploration)

Safety · Observation · Baseline skill check

  • Goal: help students get comfortable with the engineering robot; observe coordination and baseline skill level.
  • Flow: safety rules → free exploration → a small positioning challenge → short recap.

Lesson 2 — Motion programming fundamentals (X / Y / Z)

Motion Blocks · Speed tuning · Checkpoints

  • Goal: learn X/Y/Z movement and complete a visible outcome (reach target checkpoints).
  • Hands-on: forward distance + 90° turn + checkpoint navigation, with speed tuning practice.

Lesson 3 — Robotic arm control (J1 / J2 / J3) + gripper

Pick & place · Position control · Debugging

  • Goal: control joints and gripper; complete a pick-and-place task without dropping objects.
  • Teaching focus: axis-by-axis learning and iterative trial-and-error.

Lesson 4 — Spoon throwing mechanics

Angle control · Wait-time tuning · Safety

  • Goal: understand how angle and wait-time affect throw strength; achieve multiple controlled throw ranges.
  • Practice: low / medium / high throws; iterate by trying, adjusting, and retrying.

Lesson 5 — Competition strategy

Rule analysis · Role division · Time optimisation

  • Goal: translate rules into an actionable match plan (task sequencing, safe movement, avoiding penalties).
  • Activity: small-group strategy design + feedback per group.

Lesson 6 — Practice / buffer / debugging

Catch-up support · Extra challenges · Friendly matches

  • Goal: reinforce earlier skills and provide extensive practice time (a rescue lesson for slower learners).
  • Activities: mini-competitions (grabbing, throwing, checkpoint runs) and informal team matches.

Lesson 7 — Match simulation (final)

Teamwork · Calm execution · Reflection

  • Goal: integrate all skills into a timed match scenario and practice collaboration.
  • Flow: teams of two → timed match → scoring → light awards → reflection on progress.

Teaching focus

  • Progressive learning: fundamentals → manipulation → throwing → strategy.
  • Hands-on programming using UCode4, with clear step-by-step examples and in-class challenges.
  • Designed for mixed-ability classrooms with accessible explanations and plenty of iteration/debugging time.
  • Emphasis on engineering thinking: decomposition, iteration, and disciplined troubleshooting.
